THE CHOICER VOICER — STREAMELEMENTS OVERLAY

Create a 1920 x 1080 StreamElements overlay. Add one Custom Widget at X=0, Y=0, width=1920, height=1080.
Paste overlay.html into HTML, styles.css into CSS, and overlay.js into JS. Save.
Add the overlay URL to an OBS Browser Source at 1920 x 1080 above gameplay.
Position your webcam within the frame at the upper left (left 60, top 70, width 480, height 340).

Includes a webcam frame, fading chat and queued follow/sub/gift/bits/tip/raid alerts.
Chat lifetime, alert duration, minimum bits/tip, command hiding and hidden bot names are in CFG at the top of overlay.js (optional Fields JSON is in the overlay.js header).
Tips show in your StreamElements currency. Community gift bombs show one alert, not one per recipient.
Mod deletions, timeouts and bans remove that chat from the overlay.
Your selected name is in overlay.html; preview.html is a demo, not the live widget.
Connect your channel to StreamElements and test using Emulate and live chat before streaming.
External Google Fonts require internet.

SETTINGS + BACKGROUND IMAGE (new)
Paste the 4th box from the overlay site ("Fields") into the widget's FIELDS tab.
Then your name, colours, background image, game window and show/hide switches are editable in StreamElements.
A background image fills the screen EXCEPT the game window (and webcam hole), which stay see-through.
Default game window: X=60 Y=110 width=1350 height=759 on a 1920 x 1080 canvas.
The background always sits underneath the overlay.

SCENE TRANSITION
This overlay plays a short transition in its own style when the scene appears.
In OBS: open the overlay's Browser Source properties and tick
"Refresh browser when scene becomes active". For the smoothest switch, set the
OBS scene transition to "Fade" at about 300 ms. Turn it off with
"Show scene transition" in the widget's Fields panel.
