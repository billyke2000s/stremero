# Classic community template assets

- Animation reference: https://coub.com/view/2o456b (the non-explicit recording / upload / play / community logo ident). The editable canvas template reconstructs the shapes and timeline rather than baking the channel text into a video.
- Drum sting: https://soundinstants.com/sound/hub-intro-sound, downloaded from its public audio link https://storage.soundinstants.com/hub-intro-sound.mp3. Trimmed from 0.5 to 3.46 seconds to remove the leading/trailing silence. This is the referenced brand sting, not an original Stremero composition or an assertion of royalty-free licensing. Users can replace or mute it.
- Font: unmodified Liberation Sans Bold and Regular, distributed under SIL Open Font License 1.1. See FONT-LICENSE.txt. Bold is the template default; Regular is available when the weight is changed. The font, its license and any uploaded replacement font are embedded in exported HTML so it keeps its appearance offline.
