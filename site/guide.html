NEW TO STREAMING? START HERE

From nothing to live, with a free overlay

An overlay is the frame around your stream: your webcam border, chat, alerts when someone follows, and so on. Everything here is free. You need about 20 minutes the first time.

1 What you need

  • OBS StudioFree streaming software for Windows, Mac and Linux. It shows your game, webcam and overlay together and sends them to Twitch or YouTube.obsproject.com ↗
  • A StreamElements accountFree. It runs your overlay online and makes alerts and chat work. Sign in with your Twitch or YouTube account.streamelements.com ↗
  • A Twitch or YouTube accountWhere your stream goes. Connect it in OBS under Settings › Stream.

Using a different streaming app? Any app that can add a Browser Source (a web page as a layer) can show these overlays.

2 Pick your overlay and make it yours

  1. Go to Browse and open any overlay you like.
  2. Click your name under the preview to change “Your cool name” to your streamer name.
  3. Under Customise, change the colours and add your socials. Press ▦ on the preview to see it over a sample game.
  4. Want to mix parts from different overlays? Press Open in editor, or use the Editor.

3 Put it in StreamElements

  1. On the overlay’s page press Copy to StreamElements. You get four boxes: HTML, CSS, JS and FIELDS.
  2. In StreamElements, create a new overlay at 1920 × 1080.
  3. Add a Custom Widget to it and make the widget fill the whole overlay (1920 × 1080, at 0, 0).
  4. Open the widget’s code editor. Replace each tab with the matching box: HTML into HTML, CSS into CSS, JS into JS, FIELDS into FIELDS. Then save.
  5. Copy the overlay’s link (its URL). You need it for OBS.

Every colour, your name and your socials can be changed later in the widget’s Fields panel in StreamElements, no code needed.

4 Add it to OBS

  1. In OBS, in your gameplay scene, add a Browser Source and paste the overlay link.
  2. Set its size to Width 1920 and Height 1080. OBS starts new browser sources at 800 × 600, so this step matters.
  3. Drag the Browser Source to the top of your sources list, so it sits above your game and webcam.
  4. Put your game capture and webcam where the overlay leaves holes. The overlay’s page tells you the exact numbers, for example “Webcam: X 48, Y 48, 480 × 300”. In OBS, right-click a source › Transform › Edit Transform to type them in.

5 Your other scenes

Every overlay comes with five scenes. Pick them with the tabs above the preview:

  • Gameplaywhile you play
  • Starting soonbefore you start
  • Be right backbreaks
  • Endingthanks for watching
  • Just Chattingbig webcam, for talking to chat

For each one you want: make a new StreamElements overlay with that scene’s code (step 3), and a new OBS scene with its link (step 4). Then you switch scenes in OBS while you’re live.

6 Scene transitions

Each overlay plays a short transition in its own style when a scene appears: curtains, pixels, ink splats and so on. To make OBS play it every time you switch:

  1. Open each overlay Browser Source’s properties and tick “Refresh browser when scene becomes active”.
  2. Set the OBS scene transition (bottom of the OBS window) to Fade at about 300 ms.

Don’t want it? Untick Show scene transition in the widget’s Fields panel.

7 Test before you go live

  1. In StreamElements, use Emulate on your overlay to fake a follow, sub, tip, cheer and raid. You should see each alert.
  2. Type a message in your own Twitch chat. It should appear in the overlay’s chat.
  3. Switch between your OBS scenes and check each one and its transition.

Help, something’s wrong

I can’t see the overlay in OBS

Check the Browser Source is at the top of the sources list and is 1920 × 1080. Make sure you pasted the StreamElements overlay link, not the editor page’s address.

The overlay is the wrong size or cut off

Your OBS canvas should be 1920 × 1080 (Settings › Video › Base Resolution), and so should the Browser Source.

Alerts don’t show

Try StreamElements’ Emulate first. If emulated alerts work, real ones will too. Check “Show alerts” is ticked in the widget’s Fields.

Chat is empty

The chat only appears when someone types, and old messages fade away after a few seconds. You can change that time in Fields.

My webcam is in the wrong place

Use the exact numbers from the overlay’s page (step 4). If you moved the webcam frame in the editor, the editor’s copy window gives the new numbers.

The transition doesn’t play

Tick “Refresh browser when scene becomes active” on that scene’s Browser Source, and check “Show scene transition” is on in Fields.
