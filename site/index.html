Panel headers (320 × 100) for your Twitch “About” page and a screen for when you’re offline. Tap one to download it.

How to use these on Twitch
  1. Panels: on your Twitch channel page, scroll below the stream and turn on Edit Panels. Add a panel, upload the image, write your text under it and press Submit.
  2. Offline screen: in Twitch, open Creator Dashboard › Settings › Channel › Brand and upload it under Video Player Banner (Twitch’s menus move sometimes, so search for “Video Player Banner” if it’s not there).
  3. The images have generic labels (About me, Schedule…). Put your own details in the panel text under them.
